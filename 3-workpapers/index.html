Lane A, Steps A14 to A18

Stage 3 takes each item as received from the client in stage 2 and processes the data point into the workpapers. It runs at the same time as stage 2, one item at a time.

Steps and actions are Phil's. Inputs, outputs, tools, level, evidence, exception and notes are first guesses by Claude (2026-11-08). Correct them.

    When Stage 3 Ends

    • Workpapers are populated incrementally as data items are received. Stage 3 is complete when Stage 2A and Stage 2B are both complete and all data items extracted from the client are in the workpapers or marked irrelevant, and the workpapers have been reviewed.
    • At the end of Stage 3, we have workpapers in which every number has been checked by the Preparer and the Reviewer, ready to key into UltraTax.
    • The incremental approach to data acquisition, data entry, and data verification is selected to make the final step quick. If you are waiting on one number on October 13, it's easy to finish the tax return if everything else is entered into UltraTax and has been pre-reviewed.
