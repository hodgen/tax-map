Stages 2, 3 (Data Collection, Workpaper Preparation)

Stage 2 requests, collects and organizes the data; stage 3 turns it into workpapers. They run simultaneously, item by item.

Stage 2 has two lanes.

  • Lane A confirms and updates everything that is visible from last year: P0 and A1 to A13 are stage 2, A14 to A18 are stage 3.
  • Lane B, the Go Fish call, looks for what is not visible and entirely in Stage 2. Each new thing Lane B finds becomes a new item in Lane A.

Stage 3 takes each item as received from the client and processes the data point into the workpapers.

Lane A · confirm and update

Visible from Last Year: Known Knowns, Known Unknowns

This lane identifies "known knowns" and "known unknowns" from the prior year tax return. Everything on the prior year income tax return must be accounted for in 2026: the income item continues to exist with perhaps a different dollar value, or the income item does not exist anymore. Name, address, number of dependents, etc. (the slowly moving items) are also confirmed.

    Lane B · Go Fish

    Not Visible from Last Year

    Transactions and assets the client did not tell us about, or did not know mattered. Income tax and gift tax. CPA judgment is the point of this lane.

      When Each Stage Ends

      • Stage 2A. Individual items are pushed through Stage 2 all the way to the end of Stage 3 as each item is received from the client. Stage 2 ends when 100% of the data items are received or noted as "not obtained" and why.
      • Stage 2B. This is complete when the CPA has had a live call with the client and has gone through the entire Go Fish questionnaire, receiving either a "does not apply" answer or discovering a new item to add to Lane A for data collection and verification.
      • Stage 3. Workpapers are populated incrementally as data items are received. Stage 3 is complete when Stage 2A and Stage 2B are both complete and all data items extracted from the client are in the workpapers or marked irrelevant, and the workpapers have been reviewed.
      • At the end of Stage 3, we have workpapers in which every number has been checked by the Preparer and the Reviewer, ready to key into UltraTax.
      • The incremental approach to data acquisition, data entry, and data verification is selected to make the final step quick. If you are waiting on one number on October 13, it's easy to finish the tax return if everything else is entered into UltraTax and has been pre-reviewed.
